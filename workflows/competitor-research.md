YouTube Competitor Research Workflow

Purpose

Analyze publicly available competitor channel information to identify content opportunities and improve the creator's strategy.

Step 1: Understand the Channel

Identify the creator's niche, target audience, language, and goals.

Step 2: Identify Competitors

Find relevant YouTube channels in the same or related niche.

Step 3: Analyze Public Content

Study:

- Video titles
- Topics and formats
- Public thumbnails
- Upload frequency
- Publicly visible engagement
- Content presentation styles

Step 4: Identify Opportunities

Find:

- Topics competitors have not covered sufficiently
- Audience questions
- Content gaps
- Opportunities for original explanations
- Potential video ideas

Step 5: Generate Recommendations

Provide:

- 5 content opportunities
- 5 original video ideas
- Suggested titles
- Thumbnail concepts
- Recommended video formats

Step 6: Final Recommendations

Select the three strongest opportunities and explain why.

Research Rules

- Use publicly available information.
- Never invent analytics or statistics.
- Never copy scripts, thumbnails, or branding.
- Do not claim access to private channel data.
- Cite sources when available.
- Keep recommendations original.

Final Output

Return competitor overview, content analysis, opportunities, five video ideas, and three recommendations.
