YouTube Content Calendar Workflow

Purpose

Create realistic weekly and monthly YouTube content calendars based on the creator's niche, audience, goals, and available production time.

Step 1: Understand the Channel

Identify:

- Channel niche
- Target audience
- Preferred language
- Video formats
- Upload frequency
- Content goals
- Available production time

Ask only for missing essential information.

Step 2: Plan Content

Generate relevant content ideas based on:

- Audience interests
- Channel niche
- Seasonal opportunities
- Relevant current topics
- Previous content performance, when provided

Avoid inventing trends or analytics.

Step 3: Create the Calendar

For every planned upload, include:

- Upload date
- Video topic
- Suggested title
- Video format (Short or long-form)
- Target audience
- Research status
- Script status
- Thumbnail status
- Editing status
- Publishing status

Step 4: Organize Production

Break each video into manageable tasks:

1. Research
2. Idea selection
3. Script writing
4. Thumbnail design
5. Video recording
6. Video editing
7. SEO optimization
8. Final review
9. Publishing

Step 5: Set Realistic Deadlines

- Consider the creator's available time.
- Avoid unrealistic daily upload schedules.
- Allow enough time for research and editing.
- Include flexible days for unexpected delays.

Step 6: Track Progress

Use these status labels:

- Not started
- In progress
- Completed
- Needs revision

Step 7: Review the Calendar

Check:

- Content variety
- Upload consistency
- Audience relevance
- Production feasibility
- Workload balance

Final Output

Provide:

1. Weekly or monthly calendar
2. Planned upload dates
3. Video topics and titles
4. Video formats
5. Production task checklist
6. Progress tracking
7. Recommendations for improvement

Important Rules

- Never assume the creator can upload daily.
- Do not invent performance statistics.
- Keep the schedule realistic.
- Allow changes when plans need updating.
- Never publish without creator approval.
