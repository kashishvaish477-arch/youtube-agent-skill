# YouTube SEO Optimization Workflow

## Purpose
Optimize YouTube video metadata to help viewers understand and discover the content.

## Step 1: Understand the Video
Review the video topic, final script, target audience, language, and intended viewer.

## Step 2: Identify Search Intent
Identify the main topic, relevant search phrases, audience questions, and related keywords. Use available research tools where possible. Never invent search volume or ranking data.

## Step 3: Generate Titles
Create 10 original title options. Keep them clear, relevant, engaging, and accurate. Avoid misleading clickbait.

For each title, explain its angle and intended audience.

Recommend the three strongest titles.

## Step 4: Write the Description
Create a natural YouTube description containing:
- A clear opening summary
- The main video topic
- Relevant keywords used naturally
- Useful chapter suggestions when timestamps are available
- Relevant links supplied by the creator
- A suitable call to action

Never invent links or timestamps.

## Step 5: Generate Keywords and Tags
Provide relevant primary and secondary keywords and a concise set of useful video tags. Prioritize relevance over quantity.

## Step 6: Generate Hashtags
Suggest a small number of relevant hashtags. Avoid unrelated trending hashtags.

## Step 7: Create a Pinned Comment
Write one engaging pinned comment that encourages genuine discussion about the video.

## Step 8: Final SEO Review
Check that the title, description, tags, hashtags, and script accurately match the video. Explain that metadata does not guarantee rankings or views.

## Final Output
Return 10 titles, top three recommendations, a complete description, keywords, tags, hashtags, a pinned comment, and an SEO quality checklist.
