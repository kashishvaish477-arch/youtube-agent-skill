YouTube Video Editing Workflow

Purpose

Create professional, engaging, and practical editing plans for YouTube videos and Shorts.

Step 1: Understand the Video

Identify:

- Video topic
- Target audience
- Video format
- Expected runtime
- Editing style
- Available footage and resources

Step 2: Review the Script

Divide the script into scenes and identify important visual moments.

Step 3: Create a Scene-by-Scene Editing Plan

For every scene, provide:

- Scene number
- Approximate timestamp
- Spoken dialogue or voiceover
- Main visual
- B-roll suggestions
- Text overlays
- Transitions
- Sound effects
- Background music suggestions

Step 4: Improve Audience Retention

Recommend:

- Appropriate visual changes
- Engaging opening visuals
- Clear storytelling
- Smooth transitions
- Relevant pattern changes
- Suitable pacing

Avoid unnecessary effects and distracting edits.

Step 5: Captions and Text

Provide:

- Important on-screen captions
- Highlighted keywords
- Suggested text placement
- Caption timing
- Readability recommendations

Step 6: Audio Direction

Suggest:

- Background music mood
- Voiceover clarity improvements
- Suitable sound effects
- Audio transition points
- Balanced audio levels

Use properly licensed or permitted audio.

Step 7: Editing Software Recommendations

Suggest suitable editing applications based on the creator's device, experience, and budget.

Do not assume access to paid software.

Step 8: Final Editing Checklist

Check:

- Video resolution
- Audio quality
- Caption accuracy
- Visual consistency
- Copyright permissions
- Scene transitions
- Final export settings

Final Output

Return:

1. Editing style recommendation
2. Scene-by-scene editing plan
3. B-roll suggestions
4. Text and captions
5. Transitions
6. Sound effects
7. Background music direction
8. Pacing recommendations
9. Export recommendations
10. Final editing checklist

Important Rules

- Do not claim to have edited a video unless an authorized tool confirms completion.
- Never recommend unauthorized copyrighted footage or music.
- Keep editing instructions practical and beginner-friendly.
- Adapt recommendations to the creator's available tools and budget.
- Prioritize storytelling and viewer experience.
