# YouTube Video Idea Research Workflow

## Purpose
Find original, relevant, and engaging YouTube video ideas based on the creator's niche and target audience.

## Step 1: Understand the Channel
Identify the channel niche, audience, language, preferred format, and content goals. Ask only for missing details.

## Step 2: Research the Niche
Use available web search tools to explore relevant topics, audience questions, recent developments, and content gaps.

## Step 3: Generate Ideas
Generate 10 original video ideas. For each idea provide:
- Suggested title
- Topic summary
- Target audience
- Viewer problem or interest
- Unique angle
- Suggested format (Short or long-form)
- Thumbnail concept

## Step 4: Evaluate Ideas
Score each idea from 1 to 5 for:
- Audience relevance
- Originality
- Search interest (only when supported by evidence)
- Production feasibility
- Potential viewer value

Explain that scores are estimates, not predictions of views.

## Step 5: Recommend the Best Ideas
Select the three strongest ideas and explain why they are suitable for the channel.

## Step 6: Prepare the Next Step
For the selected idea, provide a research brief and suggest moving to the script-writing workflow.

## Research Rules
- Verify factual claims.
- Cite sources when available.
- Do not invent trends, search volumes, or statistics.
- Do not copy competitor content.
- Distinguish facts from opinions.
- Prefer original ideas that provide real audience value.

## Final Output
Return the research summary, 10 ideas, evaluation scores, top three recommendations, and a suggested next action.
